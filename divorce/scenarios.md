# Possible Scenarios

When I tell Arina about my intent, the first branch is whether she agrees to handle the legal divorce remotely.

## Decision Tree

1. Arina agrees to a remote divorce procedure.
    1. We decide how to live after the legal divorce.
        1. Continue living as a family.
        1. Separate in daily life too.
            1. She stays on the island.
                1. Liza lives half of the time with me and half of the time with her.
            1. She leaves the island.
                1. She takes Liza with her.
                1. Liza stays with me.
    1. We decide how to document Liza's guardianship after the divorce.
        1. Guardianship arrangement matches the living arrangement.
        1. Guardianship arrangement needs a separate written agreement.
2. Arina does not agree to a remote divorce procedure.
    1. Need to understand what she objects to.
        1. The divorce itself.
        1. The remote procedure.
        1. The proposed living arrangement.
        1. The proposed arrangement for Liza.
    1. Need to choose the next legal or practical step based on her objection.
