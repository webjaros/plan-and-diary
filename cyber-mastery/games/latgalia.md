## World generation
1. Digging the floor may result in falling into the underlying area
2. Air, water, and skies are also a type of terrain
3. There is level 0. Like sea level. The higher/lower the area is, the more change of getting certain properties.
4. There will be mandatory objects. Some of them will have permanent coordinates.
5. There must be a possibility to generate additional tiles to an area.
6. Generate mobs and other world components 
7. Educate myself in cellular automata and other algorithms to understand the basics and avoid reinventing the wheel.
8. Which form does the world have?
9. 1 tile = one step? 1 level = person’s height? Depending on these understand and plan distances in the world.
10. Any floor of any building is also an area which must be generated.
11. Zero coordinates must be in the center of the world. Without minuses we won’t be able to generate the world to all directions.
12. There have to be 000,001, etc.
13. It should be possible to generate area surrounded by walls or mountains and having an entrance.
14. There cannot be grass block below another grass block.
15. A horse can jump over the holes in terrain. Player must push a button for this. And there is a skill. At 100% no button needed anymore.
16. Define out of how deep hole a P or a horse can climb.
17. How to avoid newbies getting into the traps of more experienced players?
