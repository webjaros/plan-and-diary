This document describes my vision of my game development and publishing company.

- Dark dungeon with bonfires in the corners of the rooms that are guarded by the monsters and must be lit by the player.

## Random ideas
- We live on level 57 of the matrix. Elites are not the evil but the good who are monitoring us. Only they have knowledge of the real world. Humanity went deeper and deeper into the level of the matrix to win more time. Each level gives x10 time boost.
- We allow players to bring some stuff from the other games. These items have attributes which do not normally exist in the current game. There should be risk, probability, limitation, and skill.
- There will be people and NPCs who don't care about other worlds, who take opportunity, and cultists who confront this.
- They have to meet a developer who tells them that they need to install another game.
